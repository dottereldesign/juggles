Prototype notes

Counting a touch

A tap can excite both sensors. The counter needs to group those signals into one touch and distinguish kicks from ground bounces. A bare piezo also needs a conditioned input to keep its voltage within the controller’s limits. Piezo sensor guide

Battery and coil fit

The XIAO’s 380 mA fast charge is within the TAC pack’s 500 mA maximum. The DFRobot pair is rated for a 2–10 mm gap, so the receiver belongs near the shell. Bench-test the electronics first. Repeated kicks require an enclosure that protects the cell from impact. XIAO specs · Cell datasheet · Coil specs
