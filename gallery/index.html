Accelerometer & gyroscope

The pictured GY-521 is one six-axis option. The tutorial uses a BMI160 board with different firmware registers. One MPU-6050 chip on the GY-521 measures both acceleration and rotation. Its accelerometer records changes in motion during contact. Its gyroscope measures how quickly the ball rotates about each of three axes.

Piezo contact sensor

The inner ceramic layer bends with its metal backing when the shell flexes. The resulting electrical pulse gives the controller a second signal for timing a touch.

The stand powers the transmitter board. Its coil creates the changing magnetic field that transfers energy across the gap between the stand and the ball.

Receiver inside the ball

This flat Qi receiver is shown on a phone. The equivalent part sits close to the outer shell in the proposed ball. Its electronics convert power from the coil into a DC supply for the charger.

Charge controller

A single-cell charger controls battery voltage and charge current. This MicroLipo example has a battery socket and a current selector. The XIAO already has a charge controller, so a separate board is only needed for controller variants without one.

Make the 100 mm shell by hand

NZ$7.53 for the materials before delivery if you buy the ball, the full foam pack and the tape roll below. With spare EVA, tape and a shipping carton at home, the ball alone is $2.35. Prices checked 24 November 2026.

  • Shell · $2.35. One solid 100 mm polystyrene craft ball, item 263100. Golding does not display a freight price for the single ball. Spotlight also lists a pair of 100 mm balls at a regular $10 per pack. Golding Craft · Spotlight two-pack
  • Carrier and pads · $2.00. Reuse a flat panel from a corrugated shipping carton ($0). A ten-pack of A4 EVA craft sheets costs $2. Cut two strips about 42 × 5 mm and layer to 2–3 mm thick. Look Sharp quotes $7 North Island or $14 South Island delivery. Look Sharp EVA sheets
  • Removable seam · $3.18. One 18 mm × 50 m roll of paper masking tape. Allow about 34 cm for one wrap with overlap. Check local store stock for pickup. Mitre 10 quotes $8 metro or $13.50 rural for standard parcels under 25 kg. Mitre 10 PAL tape

Cut and fit the layers

  1. Mark the equator with a paper strip and split the empty sphere with a fine serrated blade. Scoop each half gradually toward an 88 mm inner diameter, leaving roughly 6 mm of wall. Do all carving with the electronics out.
  2. Cut an 80 mm disc from roughly 2.8 mm corrugated card. Add three pairs of small scrap-EVA ledges around the rim to centre the disc, leaving its wiring notch clear. Fit the two EVA strips on the board side. Carve a shallow flat seat for the 43 mm receiver at the charging face and dry-fit it against the dock.
  3. Test the fit with a dummy load first. Align the two halves and wind the tape once around the seam. Fold the last centimetre back on itself to make a pull tab. Peel it open to adjust the fit. Use this shell for bench fit and gentle hand-motion tests.

For powered bench tests and the piezo input-protection circuit, see the wiring tutorial.
